PATCH NOTES v_1.3.1: Strategy, Total Immersion, and the Arrival of ASOBI★MAWARI-TAI!

Hello, Holo Hunger Games managers!

We're back with a major update that completely redefines the tactical experience inside the simulator. We’ve been polishing combat systems, the UI, and adding strategic depth that will change how you plan every move.

Plus, we're bringing a very special addition to the idol roster!

Here is the complete breakdown of everything arriving in the game:

Progress & Tactical Decisions

Route Selection System (Branching Map): You are no longer at the mercy of pure luck. As you advance floors, you’ll clearly see available route options (Combat, Elite, Rest, Altar, or Shop) to plan your path to victory.

Rest & Interaction Nodes (Campfires and Altars): Key tactical points have been implemented on the map for resource recovery, meditation, and risk/reward events. Will you risk sacrificing something for greater power?

Deeper & More Tactical Combat

Enemy Intent Indicators (Combat Telegraphing): No more going in blind! You will now receive clear visual warnings regarding the enemy's intentions for the next turn, allowing you to counterattack or defend strategically.

Tactical Inventory & In-Combat Consumables: Expanded usage of tactical consumable items during turns. Use smoke bombs, poisons, or vials at just the right moment to turn the tide of a critical battle.

Full Relic Implementation: All active and passive relics (adrenaline_shot, emergency_kit, strength_ring, crit_glasses, idol_shoes, etc.) are 100% operational with their mechanical effects up and running!

New Generation: ASOBI★MAWARI-TAI!

The streets, labs, and outer space are shaking with the official arrival of Hololive's newest generation. You can now recruit and lead Achichi Mela, Hyakuto Kyoko, Suzuna Tsuzuri, and Sorashina Sopia inside the simulator!

High-End Visual Rework & Aesthetics

Gradient Panels with Neon Borders: Main containers and floating floor headers feature a clean, modern, and visually striking interface.

Dynamic Route Cards: New option cards with hover elevation effects, color coding based on encounter type, and glowing risk gems.

Overhauled Combat HUD: Smooth health bars, action buttons styled like command cards, and immersive effects designed for complete arena immersion.

Stability & Bug Fixes

Item & Shop Normalization: Improved robustness in loading and validating items across merchant shops and treasure chests.

Click Stabilization: Fixed the annoying UnboundLocalError triggered when clicking on empty spaces in the RPG node view. Clean, uninterrupted navigation!
