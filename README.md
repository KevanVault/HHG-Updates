🚀 PATCH NOTES v_1.3: Massive Content Update & Improvements!

Hello everyone, Holo Hunger Games fans!
We are bringing one of the biggest updates to date.
We’ve been polishing the gameplay experience, adding deep management systems, and optimizing overall performance to make everything run smoothly.

Here is the complete list of everything coming to the simulator:

🛠️ Bug Fixes

	• Goodbye UI issues: Fixed the annoying scroll bug that affected RPG mode, the wardrobe, concert management, and streaming management.
	Navigating through menus is now completely smooth!

🌟 New Features & Systems

	• 🌍 Language Localization System: The game is now multilingual! English (en), Spanish (es), and Japanese (jp)
	have been officially added so you can enjoy all the VTuber lore and slang tailored to perfection.

	• ⚡ Energy System: Manage your idols' effort to get the most out of their training and streams without letting them collapse from exhaustion.

	• 🏋️‍♂️ New Rooms in The Hideout:

		• Training Room: Ideal for boosting XP gains and improving combat ratios.
		• Relaxation Room: The perfect space to fully recover energy and strengthen bonds.

	• 💖 Affinity & RPG Quests System:

		• Your idols now have an affinity system that unlocks exclusive bonuses in RPG mode.
		• Level up relationships to unlock special dialogues and exclusive side quests!

	• 🔮 Total Relic System Rework:

		• Both acquisition methods and tactical usage have been redesigned, splitting them into passive and active relics.
		• The Trophy Room has been enabled to manage your collection, alongside unique representative images for each one.

💻 Launcher & Automatic Updater

	• No more downloading the full game every time there's a patch.

	• We’ve added a custom launcher! It detects updates and allows you to update the game with a single click, fixing bugs cleanly and quickly.
	Plus, it includes an integrated news board so you can stay up-to-date with the latest development news.
